Hi, I'm Louis Luo, a Software Engineer based in Tokyo with over 10 years of experience in software development, primarily focused on iOS application development. In recent years, I have expanded my focus to Generative AI, AI Agents, and cloud-based AI application development.

I have over 10 years of experience in iOS development, including approximately 10 years with Objective-C and 4 years with Swift. Throughout my career, I have been involved in various stages of application development, from early planning and UI/UX design to implementation, testing, and release.

In addition to development, I have experience working with project stakeholders through tools such as email and JIRA to clarify requirements, specifications, and technical decisions. I also evaluate technical options and propose practical solutions based on project requirements.

I also have around 5 years of experience with Python and web technologies, including JavaScript, FastAPI, and Next.js.

More recently, I have been focusing on building applications powered by Generative AI, LLMs, RAG, and AI Agents. I have independently designed and developed multiple AI applications and AI Agents, incorporating technologies such as vector search, RAG pipelines, memory management, and multi-agent collaboration.

My AI application development also extends beyond LLM integration. I work on the surrounding engineering infrastructure required to turn AI capabilities into practical applications, including backend development with FastAPI, web applications with Next.js, containerized development and deployment with Docker, cloud infrastructure using AWS, and knowledge-update pipelines for RAG-based systems.

My current focus is on combining my long-term software engineering experience with Generative AI, AI Agents, and cloud technologies such as AWS and Azure to build practical AI applications and solutions that can be integrated into real products and business workflows.

I believe my strength lies in bridging software engineering, AI, and product development—turning emerging technologies into applications that provide real value to users.

I use this blog to document my experiments, technical research, AI application projects, and lessons learned along the way.
